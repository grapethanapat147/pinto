# PIN-0032 — New-seller checklist

**Type:** Feature · **Phase:** 9 · **Status:** Review

Spec: `.codex/specs/daily-brief.md`, Q7.

Three steps on Today, owner only, each derived from data: a channel has synced; cost coverage
is 100%; then a link to Money. Hides when the first two are done, or when dismissed.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Each step's tick comes from data, not from a stored "done" flag.
- [x] Staff never receive it.
- [x] `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run build`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` pass.

## Review notes

### What changed
- `app/components/OnboardingChecklist.tsx`: three steps at the top of Today. The ticks are read
  from data already on the page — step 1 from `metrics.asOf.synced` (a channel has synced),
  step 2 from the 30-day period not being `partial` (every line sold in 30 days has a cost) —
  so no new query and nothing stored for "done". Step 3 "ดูกำไรจริง" is shown locked; the
  checklist disappears once steps 1 and 2 are both true, as the spec says.
- "ปิด" reuses `action_snoozes` with the key `checklist:onboarding` and `until` 9999-12-31, so
  no migration. `RULES.checklist` is owner-only, so staff get 403 trying to close it.
- `page.tsx` only reads the dismissal for owners; staff are passed "closed" and the view also
  checks `canSeeFinance` — two layers.

### Spec wording, resolved
"Cost coverage is 100%" is taken as the 30-day figure the Money view shows. A product costed
**without** the one-time fill therefore keeps step 2 unticked until its old lines age out —
that is the honest reading, and the fill (PIN-0028) is offered exactly to avoid it.
