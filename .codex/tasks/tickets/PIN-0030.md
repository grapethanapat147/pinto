## Review notes

### Computed now
- **Headings:** orders (today's count, "วันที่ …" when the data is not today's), unread
  messages, product count, distinct customers in 60 days. The fixed kickers keep only words.
- **Today quick-work notes:** unread messages, products low or out, today's ad spend.
- **Stock tiles:** sellable / low / out from the same statuses the table shows; stock value
  **at cost** — owner only, since cost is the owner's (PIN-0028), with a note when a product is
  uncosted. "สั่งเพิ่ม" list uses the brief's quantity (shared `restockQuantity()`).
- **Inbox tiles:** messages today, unread with chat count.
- **Growth tiles:** today's ad spend; ROAS = sales of the advertising channels ÷ their spend.
  The "สูงกว่าเป้า 0.34" line is gone — there is no target.
- **Growth product cards:** top 3 by this week's sales against last week's.
- **Today chart:** 8 weeks of profit vs cost of goods (`weeklySeries`), replacing six
  hard-coded months when only 60 days of orders exist.
- **Calendar:** the anchor's month, laid out under the right weekday (the old grid had no
  offset), dots on expected payout dates, the anchor's day selected.
- `restock_suggestions` and `product_opportunities` are no longer read; deprecated in place.

### Labelled "ตัวอย่าง" (no data exists)
Payout rail, payout table, เงินรอโอน / เงินพร้อมถอน (Today and Money), inbox response time and
chat sales, ad-attributed sales and ad profit, campaigns table, customer segments and regions,
and the three "Pinto แนะนำ" panels. They keep their stored figures, visibly as examples.

### Found while verifying
- **Per-product profit reached staff.** The new product cards showed "กำไรก่อนโฆษณา ฿X / 7
  วัน"; with units sold, that gives away unit cost, which PIN-0028 made owner-only. Staff now
  see 7-day sales instead. The existing staff test only caught it when an uncosted product
  happened to rank top 3 (it depends on the seed's time of day), so it failed in unrelated
  falsification runs; a new assertion checks every staff card, every time — and fails with
  the fix reverted.

### Verification
- 75/75; tsc and lint clean. New tests compute expectations in SQL: kickers vs real counts,
  stock tiles vs the Stock table, stock value at cost and absent for staff, every no-data tile
  flagged and every computed one not, the last chart bar = the 7 days to the anchor and the
  8 bars together = the 56 days, the calendar's month, restock quantities = the brief's, the
  top trend's percentage.
- Falsified 6 ways (literal kicker, example flag dropped, ROAS target restored, weeks shifted,
  restock disagreeing with the brief, stock value reaching staff) — all caught.
- All seven views at 1440 / 1024 / 760 / 390: no horizontal overflow; 8 chart labels do not
  overlap at 390.
