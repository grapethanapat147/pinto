**No data behind them at all:** payouts (rail, table, เงินรอโอน, เงินพร้อมถอน), the six-month
chart (only 60 days exist), the August-2569 calendar and its event dots, customer segments,
regions, per-campaign spend/revenue, inbox response time and chat sales, "ยอดขายจากโฆษณา"
(no attribution), the ROAS "สูงกว่าเป้า 0.34" (no target), and the three "Pinto แนะนำ" panels.

The topbar already carries a "ข้อมูลจำลอง" pill on every view.

## Open questions (recommendations first)
- **Q1 Derivable figures** — compute all of the first table. *Recommend: yes.*
- **Q2 Six-month chart** — *recommend: last 8 weeks, weekly profit vs cost of goods, computed.*
  Alternative: remove it.
- **Q3 Calendar** — *recommend: show the anchor's month; dots on the expected payout dates*
  (from the payouts rows). Alternative: remove it.
- **Q4 Figures with no data** — *recommend: a small "ตัวอย่าง" label on each such block, and
  delete the ROAS target line (there is no target).* Generating fake-but-consistent payouts,
  segments or campaigns would be more invented data, which PIN-0027 moved away from.
