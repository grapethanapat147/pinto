# PIN-0027 — Profit computed, with its coverage

**Type:** Feature · **Phase:** 8 · **Status:** Done

Spec: `.codex/specs/cost-and-profit.md`, Q3 and the coverage design.

## Blocked on a decision, found before writing any code

Computing profit honestly from the orders that actually exist gives a number that cannot sit
on the same card as the figures around it:

| | from real orders | shown on screen today |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| orders | 6 | 284 |
| sales | ฿10,140 | ฿126,840 (today) · ฿3,246,780 (30 days) |
| COGS, costed lines only | ฿2,670 | — |
| ad spend | ฿16,800 | — |
| **profit** | **≈ −฿9,330** | ฿48,720 |

The seeded headline tiles describe a busy shop. The orders table describes six orders. And ad
spend was seeded at the busy shop's scale, so against the real six orders it exceeds sales
and profit comes out **negative**.

**One figure cannot be made honest while its neighbours stay fictional.** A computed profit
of −฿9,330 printed beside a seeded ฿126,840 in sales is worse than the current state, not
better. Either the whole Today card derives from orders, or none of it does — and that is a
question about what the demo is for, not a technical one. Asked of เกรพ before building.

## เกรพ chose option ก (2026-10-06)

Keep the demo the busy shop it always claimed to be, but make every figure computed from
orders. So the seed grows to sixty days of generated history around the six hand-made orders.

### Three things the first measurement of that history turned up

**1. The demo would have gone blank two days after deploy.** The old tiles never aged because
they were constants. Computed tiles filter on `placed_at`, and the seed anchors dates to the
moment it runs — so against the wall clock, "วันนี้" holds the seed day's orders on the seed day
and nothing at all from the next morning. Shipping option ก with wall-clock windows would have
produced an empty dashboard within a day.

The fix is the correct behaviour for a sync-based tool anyway: **report up to the last
successful sync, not up to the wall clock.** You cannot report on orders you have not fetched.
The anchor is the newest `channel_health.last_synced_at`; every window ends there; and the
"อัปเดตล่าสุด" label — currently the literal `10:42 น.` — shows it. When the anchor is not
today, the card says which day its data is from instead of letting "วันนี้" mean a day that has
passed. For a live shop the anchor tracks the clock; for the static demo it says honestly when
its data is from.

**2. "Today" was compared with a whole yesterday.** Measured at 15:00, today-so-far showed
−20% against a full yesterday — the classic partial-period bug. Today is now compared with
**yesterday up to the same minute**.

**3. The margin was implausible.** Costs set at 35–40% of price gave a 54% net margin, which
no marketplace home-goods reseller has. Raised to about 55% of price, which lands net margin
near 40% — in line with what the old tiles claimed — while staying an honest upper bound
because one product is still uncosted and fees are not yet known.

## Scope
1. Profit on Today and Money computed from lines: sales − COGS − ads. Fees and shipping are
   zero until the marketplace supplies them, and the screen says profit excludes them.
2. Every profit figure carries **coverage**: the share of revenue whose items have a cost.
3. Below 100%, the figure is labelled as partial and names what is missing.
4. The three stored profit columns stop being read; deprecated in place.

## Review notes

### What changed
- **Seed** (`scripts/seed.ts`, new `scripts/generate-history.ts`): sixty days of
  deterministic history around the six hand-made orders — 14,624 orders, 16,487 lines, 120
  `ad_spend_daily` rows. Ads run ~17–21% of TikTok/Shopee sales; LINE has none.
- **Migration** `drizzle/0007_rapid_inhumans.sql`: `ad_spend_daily` (shop, channel, Bangkok
  day, spend), unique per shop/channel/day. Additive only.
- **`db/performance.ts`** (new): one grouped pass over orders and one over lines for all six
  windows (three periods × current/previous), ads prorated only where a comparison window
  takes part of a day.
- **Views**: Today card, snapshot tiles, channel table, Money hero/waterfall/note, Orders
  count tiles and the quick-pack note now come from orders. `listOrders` returns the newest
  100, and the Orders view says how many it is not showing.

### Found and fixed along the way
1. **Hidden time-zone bug in the seed.** `at()` used the machine's zone for times and
   `dateOnly()` used UTC for dates; before 07:00 Bangkok they disagreed about which day it
   was. Harmless while every figure was a constant, wrong as soon as ad spend is joined to
   orders by day. Both now pin +07:00 explicitly.
2. **Hand-made orders could be in the future.** Pinned to 09:33–10:36, a seed run at 08:00
   put them after the last sync, outside every window. They now keep their spacing but end
   six minutes before the seed runs.
3. **A built-in +17% "growth".** The generator scaled today's orders against a 20-hour day
   while spreading every other day across 23.5 hours, so today-so-far beat
   yesterday-at-the-same-time by ~17% on every seed, by construction. Measured (135 today vs
   141 yesterday at the same minute after the fix) and fixed.
4. **Unaliased aggregate columns.** The six windows' expressions differ only in bound
   parameters, so they shared a column name. Real D1 returns arrays and was correct; the test
   wrapper returns objects and silently collapsed them (6,651 orders "today"). Every column
   is now aliased, which is correct under both.
5. **Literals on the Money view that the data did not support**: "↑ 12.4%", "ข้อมูล 98.6%",
   and "มีเพียง 4 ออเดอร์ที่รอค่าขนส่งจริง" — there is no shipping data at all. Replaced with
   the computed change, the computed coverage, and a note naming the uncosted product and
   saying fees and shipping are not yet deducted. The waterfall's bar heights were fixed in
   CSS too; they are now to scale.
6. **The delta arrow was always ↑.** It now follows the sign, and a zero or negative baseline
   reads "—" rather than a meaningless percentage.

### Verification

  ฿99,880 sales, profit ฿39,817 — identical on screen. Channel rows sum exactly to the day in
  orders, sales and profit; waterfall ฿83.2k − ฿36.6k − ฿12.8k = ฿33.8k.
- 30-day margin 40%, coverage ~80% (one product, ผ้าปูโต๊ะ Linen Sand, uncosted on purpose).
- No horizontal overflow and no clipped text on Today, Money or Orders at 1440, 1024, 760
  and 390.
- Every new test falsified by breaking what it guards — 10 mutations, 10 caught. One test
  ("channels add up to the day") was initially tautological — it compared the channels with
  a day total that is itself their sum — and was rewritten to compare with independent SQL.
